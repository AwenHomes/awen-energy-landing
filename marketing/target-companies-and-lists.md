## 8. TIMING & SEASONALITY

### High Activity Periods

**Spring (March-May)**
- Homeowners notice systems underperforming as production should increase
- Tax season → thinking about solar investment gone wrong
- Good time for outreach

**Summer (June-August)**
- Peak production season = failures are more obvious
- Homeowners getting high electric bills despite having solar
- Very active social media

**Fall (September-November)**
- End of year push
- Homeowners wanting to resolve before year-end
- Black Friday promotion timing ← YOU ARE HERE

**Winter (December-February)**
- Slower but still viable
- Focus on warm-weather states
- Plan content for spring push

### Monthly Patterns
- **Month-end**: Bills arrive, people notice solar problems
- **Weekends**: More social media activity from homeowners
- **Monday mornings**: Catch-up from weekend realizations
