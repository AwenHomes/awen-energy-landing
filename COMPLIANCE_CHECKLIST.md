# Daily DNC Compliance Checklist for Awen Energy

## Before Making ANY Call or Sending ANY Text

Use this checklist for EVERY lead before contact:

### Pre-Contact Verification (Complete for EVERY Lead)

- [ ] **Consent Verified**: Lead has checked ALL THREE consent boxes (Phone, Text, Email)
- [ ] **Consent Date**: Consent was obtained within the last 90 days (or lead has engaged recently)
- [ ] **Internal DNC Check**: Phone number is NOT on our internal Do Not Call list
- [ ] **Records Available**: I have access to the original consent form with timestamp
- [ ] **Proper Time**: Current time is between 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM in the LEAD'S time zone
- [ ] **Caller ID**: My caller ID is properly configured with callback number

### During the Call (Required Disclosures)

Within the first 10 seconds, state:
- [ ] "Hi, this is [YOUR NAME]"
- [ ] "Calling from Awen Energy"
- [ ] "Regarding your solar warranty recovery inquiry"

### If Lead Requests to Be on Do Not Call List

**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:**
1. [ ] Stop all sales talk immediately
2. [ ] Verbally confirm: "I'm adding you to our Do Not Call list right now"
3. [ ] Note the date, time, and method of request
4. [ ] Add to internal DNC list within 1 hour
5. [ ] Do NOT ask why or try to continue the conversation

### After Every Contact Attempt

- [ ] Log the outcome in CRM/spreadsheet
- [ ] Note: Answered, Voicemail, No Answer, Wrong Number, Opt-Out Request
- [ ] If Wrong Number: Add to internal DNC list
- [ ] If Opt-Out: Complete opt-out procedure immediately

## Common Scenarios & Responses

### Scenario: "How did you get my number?"
**Response**: "You submitted an inquiry through our website at AwenEnergy.com requesting information about solar warranty recovery services and provided consent for us to call you."

### Scenario: "I didn't give you permission to call me!"
**Response**: "I apologize for any confusion. Would you like me to add you to our Do Not Call list right away?"
- Then follow opt-out procedures

### Scenario: "Take me off your list!"
**Response**: "Absolutely, I'm adding you to our Do Not Call list right now. You won't receive any more calls from us. Is there anything else I can help you with today?"
- Document the request immediately
- No grace period - this is their last contact

### Scenario: "What if I want to call you back later?"
**Response**: "Of course! You can reach us at [PHONE] or email us at info@awenenergy.com. Your number will remain on our Do Not Call list, so we won't call you, but you're always welcome to reach out to us."

### Scenario: Wrong number / Person never inquired
**Response**: "I apologize for the mistake. I'll make sure this number is removed from our system right away."
- Add to internal DNC list immediately

## Red Flags - STOP and Escalate

If you encounter ANY of these situations, STOP and consult with management/legal:

🚩 Lead claims they never submitted the form
🚩 Lead threatens legal action
🚩 You're unsure if consent was properly obtained
🚩 Lead is on National DNC Registry and you're unsure about consent
🚩 Consent form is missing required checkboxes
🚩 Lead is in a state with special requirements you're unfamiliar with
🚩 Technology system shows lead requested opt-out but you're seeing conflicting information

## Emergency Contact Stoppage

If a lead mentions any of these keywords, IMMEDIATELY stop contact and escalate:

- "Lawyer" / "Attorney"
- "Lawsuit" / "Sue"
- "FCC" / "FTC" / "TCPA"
- "Class action"
- "Harassment"

**Actions:**
1. Stop contact immediately
2. Add to DNC list
3. Document everything about the interaction
4. Notify management within 1 hour
5. Preserve all records related to this lead
6. DO NOT contact again without legal clearance

## Quick Reference: Time Zones

When calling, verify lead's time zone:

- **Eastern**: GMT-5 (EST) / GMT-4 (EDT)
- **Central**: GMT-6 (CST) / GMT-5 (CDT)
- **Mountain**: GMT-7 (MST) / GMT-6 (MDT)
- **Pacific**: GMT-8 (PST) / GMT-7 (PDT)
- **Alaska**: GMT-9 (AKST) / GMT-8 (AKDT)
- **Hawaii**: GMT-10 (HST)

**Safe Calling Hours**: 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM in the LEAD'S time zone
